# In-Woo Park

Hi, I’m Inwoo Park (published as In-Woo Park in scientific articles), and I am a postdoctoral researcher at IMAS, UTAS, Australia.

I am interested in ice‑dynamics modeling using physics‑based software such as ISSM (Ice Sheet and Sea‑level System Model).

I have worked on:
* Initialization of ice‑sheet models.
* Investigating how surface melting influences future ice‑sheet projections.

I am currently working on:
* Developing sub–ice‑shelf melting parameterizations that are not overly simplified but remain physically realistic.
* Subglacial hydrology systems.
* Simulating ice damage and coupling calving parameterizations with damage evolution.
